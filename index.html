Island Discovery

A comprehensive 10-day journey through the best of Sri Lanka — culture, nature, wildlife, and pristine beaches.

Tour Overview

This carefully crafted 10-day journey takes you from the bustling streets of Colombo to the ancient wonders of the Cultural Triangle, through the misty highlands of Ella and Nuwara Eliya, and down to the golden southern coastline.

Experience private safaris in Yala, ride the world-famous scenic train through tea country, explore the UNESCO-listed Galle Fort, and unwind on some of the most beautiful beaches in the Indian Ocean.

Tour Highlights

Climb the iconic Sigiriya Rock Fortress at sunrise
Ride the Kandy to Ella scenic train
Private jeep safari in Yala National Park
Explore the Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic
Walk through tea plantations in Nuwara Eliya
Whale watching in Mirissa
Discover Galle Fort's colonial architecture
Authentic Sri Lankan cooking experience

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 1

Arrive in Colombo

Airport pickup, transfer to your boutique hotel in Colombo. Evening orientation walk through the Fort district and dinner at a local restaurant.

Day 2-3

Sigiriya & Dambulla

Drive to the Cultural Triangle. Climb Sigiriya at sunrise, explore the ancient Dambulla Cave Temple, and visit a rural village for an authentic lunch experience.

Day 4-5

Kandy

Visit the Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic, explore the Royal Botanical Gardens, and attend a traditional Kandyan dance performance.

Day 6-7

Ella & Nuwara Eliya

Scenic train ride through tea country, hike to Little Adam's Peak, visit a tea factory, and enjoy the cool highland climate.

Day 8

Yala National Park

Full-day private safari in Yala — spot leopards, elephants, sloth bears, and incredible birdlife in one of the world's most biodiverse parks.

Day 9

Galle & Mirissa

Explore the UNESCO-listed Galle Fort, then head to Mirissa for a relaxing evening by the beach.

Day 10

Departure

Morning at leisure on the beach. Optional whale watching tour. Transfer to Bandaranaike International Airport for your departure.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this tour suitable for children?

This tour is suitable for children aged 6 and above. We can adjust the pace and activities to accommodate families. For younger children, we recommend our dedicated Family Adventure tour.

Can I customize this itinerary?

Absolutely. This is a suggested itinerary that we tailor to your preferences, budget, and travel dates. Add extra days, swap destinations, or change accommodation levels — it's your journey.

What's the best time of year for this tour?

This tour works well from December to April when the west and south coasts are dry. For May to September, we can adjust the beach portion to the east coast (Trincomalee/Arugam Bay).

What type of accommodation is included?

We use a mix of boutique hotels, eco-lodges, and premium properties. All accommodation is handpicked for location, comfort, and character. Upgrade options are available.
